Why we exist

Our kind of motorcycle group

We ride, trade knowledge, make friends, and help people find their footing without asking them to perform somebody else's idea of biker culture. Some of us have ridden for decades, while others are newly endorsed and still learning. What matters is showing up for the people you ride with.

What we expect

Call people what they ask to be called. Pay attention to boundaries. Ride in a way that does not turn somebody else's day into an emergency. Nobody here needs to prove how tough, fast, loud, experienced, or mechanically gifted they are. Bring what you know, ask about what you don't, and notice when somebody could use a hand.
