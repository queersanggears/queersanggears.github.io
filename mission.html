Values

No corporate mission statement. Just a clear idea of how we ride and how we treat people.

The deal

The road has enough hazards. We don't need to become hazards to one another.

Not set in stone

The club should change with the people doing the work. Members get a say in rides, events, leadership, and what we build next.

If something is not working, say so. If something is missing, help us make it.
