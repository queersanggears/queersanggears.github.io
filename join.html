Membership requirements

Membership is open to Oklahoma residents who:

  • Are 21 or older
  • Own a motorcycle
  • Have a motorcycle endorsement

If you meet those requirements, email us and tell us a little about yourself and what you ride. We'll point you toward the next ride, meetup, or Discord conversation.
