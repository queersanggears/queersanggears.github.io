Officers

People helping organize rides, events, communication, and the day-to-day work of the alliance.

President

She/Her

Froggy

Our fearless leader, Froggy supervises Queers & Gears in all its affairs, including media outreach, club management, fundraising efforts, and social media content generation. In her spare time, when she's not slicing keratin or riding her bike, you can find her on the lake with her paddle board, paddling her problems away. Froggy has been riding dirt bikes since she was 10, and street bikes for a year.

Vice President

She/Her

Sparky

Our animated VP, Sparky assists the President in club management and media outreach, and steps in as ranking leader if Froggy is indisposed. A total jokester, if she isn't riding, she's enjoying the peaceful life, either grilling or fishing. Sparky has been riding motorcycles for 5 years.

Sergeant-at-Arms

She/It/He

Songdog

The right hand of the President, Songdog assists Froggy with club management and media outreach, and is also the club photographer and safety officer. A travel enthusiast, in its free time it likes to plan road trips to remote destinations. Songdog has been riding motorcycles continuously for two years.

Secretary/Treasurer

She/Her

Scherzo

The oil that keeps us running smoothly, Scherzo undertakes many clerical tasks for the club, including note-taking, keeping up with our paperwork, and managing club funds. A shenanigan enthusiast and popcorn connoisseur, she is also the queen of standstill bike drops.

Road Captain

They/Them

Ochre

Our adventurous Road Captain, Ochre leads group rides and collaborates with our Tail Gunner to plan trip routes. A novice binomial nomenclature enthusiast, this lover of Latin will also sneak their way into your heart by challenging you to an arm wrestle just to hold hands.

Tail Gunner

He/Him

Scooter

As our Tail Gunner, the ever supportive Scooter brings up the end of the pack and collaborates with our Road Captain to plan trip routes. When he's not developing software, you can find him collecting physical media or adventuring off road, on either two wheels or four. Scooter has been riding motorcycles for 18 years.
